# Experience Principles — the synthesis

> The goal is not a better build. The goal is a better *feeling*.
> When someone visits, they should feel **"this is premium"** — not "this looks like X."

This document is the design contract for the elevation. It fuses three things:
**Oryzo's experience quality** + **Do Amore's product domain** + **our ring configurator**.

---

## I. Oryzo's principles — abstracted, never copied

What makes the Lusion/Oryzo experience feel extraordinary, distilled into rules:

1. **One subject, one light.** Everything else is atmosphere. Here, the ring is the only luminous object; the room stays dark so the metal and stone *are* the light.
2. **Motion is a breath, not a transition.** Easing is always `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)` / `(0.22,1,0.36,1)`. Idle states oscillate like breathing. Nothing eases linearly. Configuration changes feel like *events*, not UI state flips.
3. **Every section is its own world.** Distinct lighting, camera language, and motion vocabulary per act (flip, drift, push, rise). The transition *between* worlds is part of the story.
4. **Scroll moves the narrative, not just the pixels.** A section earns its scroll-length by transforming. If scrolling past it changes nothing, it is too long.
5. **Confidence through restraint.** Huge type, generous negative space, few elements, no decoration that doesn't increase perceived value. Cut the clever effect that competes with the subject.
6. **Reveal in stages.** Eyebrow → headline (per-word/char) → body → supporting detail, each on its own beat. Never everything at once.
7. **Craft in the micro.** Magnetic buttons, char-stagger headlines, blur-in text, a gold progress hairline, sub-pixel timing. The details are the message.

## II. Do Amore's domain — the substance that earns the emotion

A luxury engagement ring is bought on **trust and meaning**, not specs alone. Do Amore wins because every element answers *"why does this matter?"*:

1. **The product has a story rooted in nature** — two bands intertwined like two paths "destined to be interwoven." The twist *is* the metaphor.
2. **Provenance is proof.** 100% recycled gold, independently certified; a ring box made of sustainably harvested wood. Specificity = credibility (28 pavé diamonds, 0.10ctw, VS, comfort-fit, resizeable).
3. **The mission is the soul.** Every ring funds a clean-water well; the buyer receives GPS coordinates and a photo of the well they made possible. *"Every proposal starts something bigger."* This is the emotional climax.
4. **Trust is explicit.** Free resizing for life, lifetime warranty, free insured shipping & 60-day returns, transparent financing.
5. **Real voices.** Specific, human testimonials — not star ratings in a vacuum.

## III. The synthesis — our rules for this site

- Keep the **dark, cinematic, ring-is-the-only-light** world (our identity). Do *not* import Do Amore's bright cream/beach aesthetic — it would dilute the confidence.
- Inject Do Amore's **substance** (provenance, mission, trust, spec truth, nature metaphor) — rendered in *our* world with *Oryzo-grade* motion.
- The narrative arc earns an emotional climax: **possibility → creation → craft → materials → provenance → our promise → the meaning of a promise → the mission (a well) → real voices → forever.**
- Every new section must pass two tests: **(1) does it move the story forward? (2) would removing it lose a feeling, not just information?** If a section is functional but not memorable, it is not done.

## IV. Narrative order (target)

1. **ActOne** — *possibility* (particles converge)
2. **Studio** — *creation* (live configurator)
3. **Craft** — *the birth of form*
4. **Materials** — *metals & stones*
5. **Provenance** *(new)* — *origin & craftsmanship*: recycled gold, certified, spec truth, the wooden box
6. **Commitment** *(new)* — *our promise to you*: resizing for life, lifetime warranty, shipping, financing
7. **Promise** — *a ring is a promise you can hold* (cream palette cleanser)
8. **Mission** *(new)* — *every ring builds a well*: impact counters, GPS-of-a-well poetry — the emotional peak
9. **Testimonials** — *real voices*
10. **Closing** — *forever* (CTA)
